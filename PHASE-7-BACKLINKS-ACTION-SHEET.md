## 🥉 TIER 3 — Free Content / Entity Links
Republish or adapt your **blog articles** here with a brand + ONE money-page link. Builds entity + referral traffic.

| Platform | Use | Anchor style |
|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn Articles** (company/personal) | Adapt "Deep Cleaning Cost in Dubai" / "Move-Out Checklist" | Brand / naked URL |
| **Medium** | Unique version of a blog guide | Brand + 1 contextual money-page link |
| **Blogspot** | Support article | Brand / partial-match |
| **Tumblr / Substack** | Short tips post linking to a guide | Brand / topical |

**Rules (important):**
- Unique content each time — never paste the same article across platforms.
- Max **1** contextual money-page link + 1 brand/homepage link per post.
- Don't repeat exact-match anchors. (See anchor mix below.)

---

## 🤝 TIER 4 — Partner & Relationship Links (highest quality)
Reach out for genuine partnerships — these are the strongest links.

- **Real-estate agencies & brokers** — they need move-in/out cleaning for clients → "recommended cleaner" link.
- **Property management companies** — building/villa community management.
- **Airbnb / holiday-home hosts & managers** — turnover cleaning synergy.
- **Interior designers / fit-out companies** — post-construction cleaning referrals.
- **Movers / relocation companies** — move-in/out cleaning cross-referral.
- **Building/community Facebook groups & resident portals** — list as trusted vendor.

> Offer value first (referral discount, reliable service), then ask for a website mention/link.

| Partial-match | "villa cleaning service in Dubai by Al Haya" | 15–25% |
| Topical/generic | "professional cleaning", "this guide" | 10–20% |
| Exact-match | "villa cleaning Dubai" | 0–5% (rare) |

## 🚫 Do NOT
- ❌ Buy bulk/cheap link packages (PBNs, fiverr "1000 backlinks").
- ❌ Use the same exact-match anchor everywhere.
- ❌ List inconsistent NAP (different phone/address per site).
- ❌ Spam comments/forums with links.
- ❌ Link every post only to the homepage.

## ✅ DO
- ✅ One consistent NAP everywhere.
- ✅ Prioritise local UAE + niche relevance.
- ✅ Build steadily (5–10 quality links/month), not in one burst.
